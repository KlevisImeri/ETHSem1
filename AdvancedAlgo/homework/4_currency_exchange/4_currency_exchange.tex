\paragraph{Goal 1.}
Design a deterministic $2^{k/2}$-competitive online algorithm $\mathcal A$
such that
\[
\forall\sigma\in[1,2^k]^T:\qquad
c_{\mathcal A}(\sigma)
\geq\frac{\mathrm{OPT}(\sigma)}{2^{k/2}},
\]
and prove that no deterministic algorithm has a smaller competitive ratio.
